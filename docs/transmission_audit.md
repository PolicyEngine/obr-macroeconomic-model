## Reading this

- **transmitting**: the shock reaches behavioural variables (consumption, investment, jobs, prices), not just the spending identity.
- **identity-only**: GDP moves but no behavioural channel does; the multiplier is missing and the result is just the mechanical add to demand.
- **dead**: neither GDP nor any behavioural channel moves.

> **A 'transmitting' verdict is about wiring, not about the answer being right.** *Gov consumption* is identity-only: GDP moves by about the shock and consumption by less than the threshold, which is what a flat multiplier of 1.0 looks like. *Gov investment*, *sterling* and *oil* are dead under the default closure: trade, jobs and prices do not move at all, and GDP and consumption move by less than a hundredth of a per cent, well under the threshold below. Their *Bus. invest.* entries are not a business-investment response. Under the default closure that column is the OBR's residual identity `IBUS = IF − GGI − PCIH − PCLEB − IH − IPRL` with total investment `IF` exogenous, so it moves only as the mirror image of real government investment `GGI = 100·GGIPS/GGIDEF` and of the investment deflator behind it. It is shown, but it does not count towards the verdict; `run_reform` labels the same government-investment shock a dead channel. Under the investment closure (the corporation tax row) business investment has its own equation and does count.

> **Before 25 Nov 2026** this page was generated under the published closure, which the module had kept after the nominal-GDP pin became the default for `run_reform`. Under that closure no quarter of a demand-closure run converges ([Limits](limitations.md), solver convergence), so the table was a difference of two non-converged solves, and it had not been regenerated since October 2026. It showed sterling at −0.05% of GDP with business investment +5.55%, oil at −0.02% and +1.08%, and Bank Rate at −0.07% and −0.03%; regenerated under the same closure on the current data those read +0.03% and −0.33%, −0.00% and +0.78%, and −0.03% and −2.05%. Neither set is a model response.

Materiality thresholds: a level response counts as a move at |Δ| ≥ 0.02% and a rate response at |Δ| ≥ 0.005pp. Responses within ±20% of the threshold are borderline: a verdict resting only on such channels is flagged **(marginal)** rather than treated as categorical.

**Summary:** 3 dead, 1 identity-only, 2 transmitting.

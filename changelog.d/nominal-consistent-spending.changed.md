A government-consumption (`CGG`) shock under the default nominal-GDP pin now also moves the imposed nominal spending totals (`CGGPS`, `CGGPSPSF`, `PSCE`) by the cash cost, so borrowing rises by that amount, the government deflator is unchanged and measured business investment no longer moves. The GDP multiplier goes from 1.01–1.05 to 1.01–1.04. `nominal_consistent=False` gives the earlier real-only shock.

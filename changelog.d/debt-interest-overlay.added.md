Added `run_reform(..., debt_interest=True)`, an opt-in, labelled overlay (default off) that makes central government debt interest respond to the extra borrowing a reform causes and to a gilt-yield shock, for conventional gilts, following the OBR's financing-component approach (EFO tables 6.12, 6.16, 6.17). Government consumption +£1.25bn a quarter then adds £8m / £61m / £230m of interest at q1 / q4 / q12 with GDP unchanged; applied to the EFO's own issuance the pricing rule is 5% to 13% above the EFO's interest on new gilts.

A shock to social benefits (`CGSB`) or other current grants (`CGOTR`) now also moves public sector current expenditure `PSCE` by the same amount under the default closure, so borrowing rises by the shock (#88). Before, the listing's subsidy residual absorbed it: borrowing did not move, profits fell by about 92% of the shock and the GDP effect was 12% to 13% smaller. `nominal_consistent=False` gives the old behaviour. A shock to any other line of `PSCE` now sets `df.attrs["subsidy_residual_warning"]` and warns.

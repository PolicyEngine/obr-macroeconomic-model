The reform-analysis bar chart was titled "Cumulative GDP Impact" but plotted the final quarter's GDP delta; it is now titled "GDP impact in <quarter>". The README's Data section pointed at a `data/` directory that does not exist (the files ship in `obr_macro/_data/`), and the Quick Start now says to run `uv sync` first and how long it takes.
`run_reform` attached the "mechanical passthrough, multiplier 1.0" warning to every demand-closure run, so income tax, NICs, VAT, fuel duty, corporation-tax receipts and Bank Rate results were labelled as accounting passthrough. It now attaches only to terms of the expenditure identity (`EXPENDITURE_IDENTITY_TERMS`: CGG, DINV, VAL, X, M, SDE), and those instruments carry a `transmission_note` describing their actual channel instead.
`run_reform` labels CONS and IF shocks as expenditure-identity passthrough too, and gives non-CGG identity terms a generic passthrough warning instead of the government-consumption one. The demo workflow's CLI job now fails when the CLI fails (pipefail), and `scripts/demo_shocks.py` stops if a solve does not converge or the rebuilt pair disagrees with `run_reform`.

Shocks to local authority spending lines (`LASBHH`, `LAOTRHH`, `LANCGA`, `DILAPR`, `LAPR`), grants abroad (`TROD`, `CGNCGA`) and public investment (`CGIPS`, `LAIPS`) now move the right borrowing aggregate under the default closure (#88). Before, a local authority spending rise lowered central and general government net borrowing by the shock, and government investment raised `CGNB` but not public sector net borrowing. Each shock now also moves the total the listing imposes (`PSCE`, `CGGPSPSF` or `PSNI`), and reform runs solve the local authority net borrowing identity (l.526) without its one unpublished term, so `LANB` and `GGNB` respond; the baseline is unchanged. `nominal_consistent=False` gives the old shock. An employer NICs shock now lowers general government net borrowing by the receipts (it was 7% more); the central/local split is still the listing's and is recorded in `df.attrs["procurement_offset_note"]`.

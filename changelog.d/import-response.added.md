Add an opt-in `import_response=True` to `run_reform`. In the listing total imports are fixed and non-oil goods imports are a residual, so a demand expansion lowered goods imports. With the flag, services imports follow the listing's own l.223 in deviation form, goods imports including oil move with import-weighted demand (l.211 weights, unit elasticity assumed), non-oil goods are that total less the listing's oil imports, and total imports are the sum. For government consumption of 1% of GDP, GDP per pound falls from 1.01–1.04 to 0.90–0.93. The default is unchanged.

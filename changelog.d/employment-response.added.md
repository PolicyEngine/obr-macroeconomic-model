Add an opt-in `employment_response=True` to `run_reform`. Total employment is set from hours inputs and does not respond to output. With the flag, the change in market sector employment from the listing's own l.145 passes one for one to total employment, so unemployment responds. A 1p basic-rate rise lowers employment by about 15,000 after 12 quarters; a government-consumption shock moves it very little, and the run carries a warning explaining why. The default is unchanged.

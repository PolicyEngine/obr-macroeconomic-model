The transmission audit (`python -m obr_macro.transmission_audit`, `docs/transmission_audit.md`) now builds and shocks its runs with `run_reform`'s own template builder, so it uses the default closure and every solve converges. It had stayed on the published closure, and the committed page dated from October 2026: it showed a 10% sterling depreciation lowering GDP by 0.05%, where the converged run gives +0.002%. Business investment under the default closure is a residual identity and no longer counts as a transmitting channel, so government investment, sterling and oil are now reported as dead, as `run_reform` already labels government investment. The page is regenerated and tests check it against the module.
